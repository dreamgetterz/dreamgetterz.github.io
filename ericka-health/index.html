Backup & restore

All entries are stored in this browser. There is no login or cloud sync. Clearing browser data, moving the file, changing the address, or switching devices can separate you from those records.

Keep backups private; they contain personal health and food records. Restoring replaces this profile only and never imports another profile’s data.

What this version does

Manual food entry with serving-based calorie and macro totals; dated weight and dose logs; one daily feeling check-in; a rotating, sourced insight; peptide knowledge, progress milestones, and Ask AI with separate local-record answers. No photo recognition, barcode lookup, or automatic nutrition estimates. Live AI answers require a working server connection; the app visibly reports when that connection is unavailable.
